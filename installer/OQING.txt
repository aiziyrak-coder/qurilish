QURAi — Qurilish intellektual platformasi
Versiya 1.1.0 · Windows uchun

=========================================================================
O'RNATISH
=========================================================================

Faylni ikki marta bosing. Dastur o'z papkangizga tushadi va ish stolida
yorliq paydo bo'ladi. Administrator huquqi kerak emas.

Joyi:  C:\Users\<siz>\AppData\Local\Programs\QURAi

Windows "Noma'lum nashriyot" deb ogohlantirishi mumkin — bu dastur
imzolanmagani uchun, ichida zararli narsa borligidan emas. "Qo'shimcha
ma'lumot" -> "Baribir ishga tushirish" ni bosing.

=========================================================================
NIMA KERAK
=========================================================================

- Windows 10 yoki 11
- Internet KERAK EMAS. Dastur to'liq kompyuterda ishlaydi.
- Hech qanday boshqa dastur, kutubxona yoki ro'yxatdan o'tish shart emas.

=========================================================================
BIRINCHI OCHILISH
=========================================================================

Dastur birinchi ochilganda namuna obyekti yaratiladi: "240 o'rinli
bolalar bog'chasi" — 20 ta ish, 25 bog'lanish, smeta, jurnal yozuvlari,
ijro hujjatlari, 72 kvartirali sotuv shaxmatkasi.

Ya'ni hamma bo'limni darhol ochib ko'rishingiz mumkin, hech narsa
kiritmasdan.

Ma'lumotlaringiz shu yerda saqlanadi:
  ...\Programs\QURAi\data\qurai.db

Bitta fayl. Nusxa olsangiz — hammasi saqlanadi. Sozlamalar bo'limida
"Zaxira nusxa" tugmasi ham bor.

=========================================================================
TIL
=========================================================================

O'zbekcha va ruscha. Sozlamalar bo'limidan almashtiriladi.
Yorug' va qorong'i ko'rinish ham bor.

=========================================================================
SERVER (shart emas)
=========================================================================

qurai-server.exe — telefondan kirish, bir necha qurilma orasida almashish
va masofadan imzolash uchun. Ishlatmasangiz ham dastur to'liq ishlaydi.

Kerak bo'lsa buyruq satrida:
  qurai-server.exe --add-user prorab "Ism Familiya" foreman
  qurai-server.exe

Keyin telefon brauzeridan kompyuter manziliga kirasiz.
Internetga chiqarilsa HTTPS beruvchi proksi ortiga qo'yish SHART.

=========================================================================
O'CHIRISH
=========================================================================

Papkadagi ochirish.cmd ni ishga tushiring. Ma'lumot bazasi
so'ralmasdan o'chirilmaydi.

=========================================================================
KAMCHILIK TOPSANGIZ
=========================================================================

TEKSHIRUV.txt faylida nimalarni ko'rib chiqish kerakligi ro'yxati bor.
Topilgan kamchilikni shu ko'rinishda yozib bersangiz, tez tuzatiladi:

  Qaysi bo'lim:
  Nima qildim (qadamma-qadam):
  Nima kutgandim:
  Nima bo'ldi:
  (bo'lsa) Ekran rasmi

Dastur o'zi yopilib qolsa yoki qotib qolsa — buni ham yozing, qaysi
amaldan keyin bo'lganini ko'rsating.
